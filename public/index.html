Marketing Studio

Marketing tools that show their working.

Map how people really buy, build a go-to-market plan from live evidence, study competitors' brand assets, find the data you need and track rivals. The AI cites its sources, and the code checks every citation.

Journey

Describe how your customer really buys, one stage at a time. Answer what you can, tag how you know it, and say "I don't know" where you don't: gaps are useful too. Everything saves in this browser, and the GTM, Distinct and Data finder tools use it.

Buying situations (optional)

Moments that make people think of the category, such as "the old one breaks" or "starting college". Up to 10.

    GTM strategy

    The tool first searches for recent news, research and trends, then builds a plan in which every recommendation points to its evidence. Ideas with no evidence are labelled as hypotheses. Each plan is also checked against the Sharp principles, as read by Sidharth.

    Distinct

    Step 1 documents the distinctive assets competitors already use: colours measured from their website code, their logos and images described by AI, and what search finds about their slogans, characters, sounds and packaging. It makes no suggestions. Step 2 checks asset ideas you bring against those competitors and your Journey.

    Step 1: Scan competitors

    Competitors' assets will appear here. Each one shows where it was seen.

    Step 2: Check your own asset ideas

    For each idea: clashes with competitors, where it fits your journey, what memory it could build, and how to test it with real people.

    Data finder

    Describe what you are planning. The tool searches for data sets, tests every link, names useful sources that are not public, and describes the data you would ideally want, with the primary research a small brand can run instead.

    Data sources, grouped by how sure we are of each link, will appear here.

    Survey size calculator

    How many completed responses you need for a survey result to be within your margin of error. Standard formula, no AI.

    Competitors

    For up to three competitors: a short brief, a dated timeline of recent moves, and what customers say. Every point cites its evidence, and quotes are kept only if they appear word for word in their source.

    Each competitor's brief, timeline and customer views will appear here. Allow about a minute per competitor.

    AI can be wrong. Every claim links to its source, so check it before you rely on it. Your journey, scans and history stay in this browser only. Do not type confidential or personal information.
